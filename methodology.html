Standard Operating Procedures & Evaluation Metrics

Benchmarking Methodology

How Moira.academic independently evaluates academic software, tests hallucination rates in AI tools, verifies privacy compliance, and benchmarks workflow efficiency.

METRIC 01

Citation Accuracy

AI literature search engines are tested against a corpus of 500 peer-reviewed papers across social sciences and STEM to measure citation accuracy and hallucination rates.

METRIC 02

Privacy & Data Governance

We audit terms of service to verify whether user uploads and unpublished manuscript drafts are excluded from LLM training sets and third-party tracking.

METRIC 03

Export & Interoperability

Tools are rated on open-standard export support (BibTeX, RIS, Markdown, LaTeX) to ensure researchers avoid vendor lock-in.

METRIC 04

Cost-to-Value Index

Pricing structures are evaluated strictly against institutional access availability, free educational tiers, and student budget sustainability.

STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE

Evaluation Standard Operating Procedure

At Moira.academic, software evaluations are conducted independently without editorial input or sponsorship influence from software vendors.

1. Testing Protocols

Every software benchmark undergoes a 3-stage evaluation process:

  • Quantitative Stress-Testing: Evaluation of search latency, parsing accuracy on mathematical equations, and PDF metadata extraction success rates.
  • Workflow Integration: Real-world scenario testing combining reference managers with writing environments (e.g., Zotero to Overleaf or Obsidian).
  • Institutional Access Audit: Direct verification of student email discounts, grant funding options, and open-access tier functionality.

2. Disclosure Policy

All affiliate links or referral metrics are explicitly disclosed with transparent site configuration parameters maintained in local storage.
