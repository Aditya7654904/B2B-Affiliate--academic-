What is Researchly? +
Researchly is a discovery and educational platform focused on software and digital resources for research, education, data, political science and productivity.
How do you select tools? +
Tools can be organized and reviewed according to features, intended users, workflows, pricing, integrations and other publicly available information. Individual articles should clearly state when information is sponsored or affiliate-supported.
Are recommendations sponsored? +
Some links may be affiliate links. Affiliate relationships do not need to change the educational purpose of the content. Relevant commercial relationships should be disclosed clearly.
Do you provide political opinions? +
The political-science section is intended for education and research. Content should distinguish documented facts, research findings, competing interpretations and opinions rather than promoting political candidates, parties or electoral choices.
Can companies submit their tools? +
Yes. You can create a future partner submission process for software companies that want their products considered for inclusion.
